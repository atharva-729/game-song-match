    How it works

    1 · Your song becomes a lineLoudness plus how busy the sound is, every 23 ms, smoothed over ~3 s.
    2 · Every game becomes a lineTension = how much the next play could swing the result, plus how much it just did.
    3 · Shapes get comparedBoth lines are stretched to 200 points and matched with a time-warp that may bend each by at most 5%. ★ Classic games get a small boost in the ranking.
    4 · Win probabilityNFL: nflverse's model. IPL: our own model, trained on every men's T20 match on Cricsheet.
